# Dunk Blueprint

An installable, offline web app for the Master Dunk Blueprint (Snap Fitness Nambour & home).

## Features
- **Train** – the plan for each day: Neural Warm-up, Shock Block, Power Engine and Structural Finish on Mon/Wed; skills on Tue; game night on Thu; on Fri you pick Light Lift or Dunk + Power (the CNS check); Sat training; Sun rest. Tap a set to tick it off and the rest timer starts on its own.
- **Lift log** – type in the weight for hex bar deadlifts, hack squats and calf raises. The app shows what you lifted last time.
- **Sleep log** – log last night's sleep each morning. The target is 8–9 h, or 9–10 h on Sunday.
- **Hoop tracker** – keeps your hoop height and clean-dunk streak, and tells you to raise the hoop 2–3" after 5 clean dunks in a row.
- **Eat** – the meal plan for each day, with the Wednesday oats swap, double carbs on training days and pre-workout/pre-game yoghurt.
- **Progress** – how much of each day you finished this week, your sleep average, lift progress and hoop-height history.
- **Rules** – the Golden Rules and the weekly flow.

## Use it on your phone
Host the folder on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → deploy from this branch), open the link in Safari or Chrome and choose **Add to Home Screen**. It works offline. Your data is saved on your device.
